Ghislain Atemezing

PhD, Semantic Web & Linked Data Enthusiast

Ghislain Atemezing is a Linked Data expert with more than 15 years of experience working on semantic web technologies. He holds a PhD in computer science from Télécom ParisTech (France) and is a recognized expert in ontology engineering, semantic technologies, and triple store systems.

He heads knowledge engineering practice at Mondeca, a Paris-based software company delivering semantic technologies, where he helps enrich clients' data sets with linked open data. He is also involved in promoting, supporting, and curating the Linked Open Vocabularies (LOV) initiative — a reusable linked vocabularies ecosystem shared by ontology experts around the globe.

For a fuller biography, publications, and contact details, visit his personal website: atemezing.org.
